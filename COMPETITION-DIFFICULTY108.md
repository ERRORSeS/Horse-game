# Competition fault difficulty and chronological training reports

Settings → Competition difficulty adds Easy, Normal and Hard. Normal is the existing probability calculation; Easy multiplies relevant fault chances by 0.55, and Hard by 1.30, with probability bounds retained. These are relative changes in chance, not percentage-point changes or guaranteed incident counts. Faults remain possible on Easy.

Interactive and quick competition execution errors, rails and refusals use the setting. The legacy whole-round rail/refusal model, eventing cross-country/arena jumping and simulated dressage execution errors also use it. Cross-country applies the multiplier once after contextual adjustments, including when profiles are cached. Player and NPC combinations use the same setting. Training probabilities, horse abilities, the value of a fault, movement-quality judging, medical rules and existing results are unchanged. Already generated/cached outcomes are retained. The preference saves per career and defaults to Normal for old or invalid settings.

The original overall training report now interleaves rider actions and regrouping between the exercises where they occurred. It uses log insertion order, including events at equal elapsed times. Jump attempts show the height recorded on that attempt; a rider action during jumping records the jump setting at that moment rather than inventing a completed jump. The original report renderer, controls and styling are retained. No action is counted as a successful exercise or grants extra training credit. Finishing twice does not duplicate entries, and old completed reports are not rewritten.

Validation: native Settings selection and verified reload; exact Normal execution/pole/refusal probabilities; Easy/Hard chance ratios; unchanged training refusal chance; 1,200 common-seed rounds per mode with nonzero Easy faults and increasing Easy → Normal → Hard fault counts; a cached cross-country profile receives exactly one multiplier; resolved effort records remain unchanged. A native actual-session test verified exercise → Crop → higher jump → regroup chronology, recorded 0.50/0.80 m heights, original report display, unchanged skills, idempotent finishing and saved reload. Existing rider-action, calendar/jump-off, dressage/eventing browser checks and 23 dressage/eventing scenarios passed. The full historical suite was not run.
