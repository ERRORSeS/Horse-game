# Competition and veterinary repair — draft validation report

This is a partial implementation of the 29 October brief, not a claim that all 82 acceptance cases are complete.

1. Jump-off qualification already checked total penalties, but earlier-starting NPCs were simulated by `showPlayerResult` even when outside the qualified pool. The pool now excludes those entries.
2. No first-round score reroll was found in the examined live path. The inappropriate NPC jump-off records were reproducible by inspection. First-round snapshots and qualification flags now persist, while nonqualifiers retain their opening-round ranking. Eliminated nonqualifiers rank behind valid finishers.
3. `showBeginJumpOff` built a new RPG with default state. It now copies tension, focus, confidence, energy and timing from the original session. Optional quiet rest or suitable warm-up changes those values once, with bounded deltas; opening the menu alone changes none.
4. Quick mode had a registration path into live sessions but no one-click session driver. `quickCompete` plays actual scene choices through the shared resolver, finalizes live scores and processes NPCs; eventing continues in phase order. It saves at completion rather than serializing after every simulated choice. A browser click produced 62.33% dressage and rank 16 of 19, on the same calendar day.
5. Pole baselines remain 30% below 1.00 m, 40% from 1.00 to below 1.30 m, and 45% at/above 1.30 m. Thus .95 uses 30%, 1.25 uses 40%; unsupported extreme heights retain the nearest outside tier. These are whole-round chances.
6. Personality uses a centered score with a 20-point adjustment budget: .15 × .20 = .03 maximum probability adjustment. Careful Front End/Careful but Slow reduce it; Rushes Fences/Trails Hind Legs increase it.
7. Tack uses the same centered budget, up to three percentage points; existing fit/control modifiers supply the signal, not purchase price.
8. Mood uses up to four percentage points; the separate medical mood can influence competition without rewriting permanent mood or Bond/QOL.
9. Luck is the existing deterministic per-entry/per-round uniform ±3 percentage-point draw. The saved pole plan controls actual fence indices; NPC jumping now also uses a whole-round plan rather than independent rail rolls at every effort.
10. The existing `T2_TRAIT_RULES` and exclusion registry remain authoritative. A queryable registry exposes them; contextual live competition choices now read relevant rules. NPCs retain seeded personalities, moods and traits. A complete trait-by-trait behavioral audit across every requested system is still outstanding.
11. Persistent transport profiles normalize the supplied loading weights (which sum to 130), retain existing histories, and independently draw travel tolerance. The unspecified nervous-traveler share is interpreted as 12%. Loading uses excellent/good/average/hesitant/difficult distributions; staff support and successful experience affect later attempts. More individual unloading/venue adaptation integration is still needed.
12. Travel greater than five hours requires a reserved overnight stay; exactly five does not. Fees are $65/$120/$175 then $55 per extra night, with an idempotent ledger. Evening/care/rest/morning stages persist. Country/continent choices expose longer trips and multiple transport legs. Full scheduling/availability validation for distant Calendar events is not yet complete.
13. General exams inspect actual active conditions and current symptoms, with 40/70/90% visibility bands and zero for no symptoms. They record observations and targeted-test recommendations without automatically confirming hidden diagnoses. Targeted exams and recovery remain in the original engine.
14. A seeded daily medical mood is stored separately from permanent mood. It clears once no symptomatic active condition remains. Show and profile displays can show Uncomfortable. The complete severity/treatment/exertion calibration matrix remains to be validated.
15. Changed: `show-session.js`, `showjumping-round.js`, `health-engine.js`, new `competition-repair.js`, asset versions in `index.html`, test wiring and tests. `script.js`, `care-fixes.js`, `care-sales.js`, `behavior.js`, `tack.js`, `management.js` and `pasture.js` remain unchanged.
16. Seeded neutral simulations: 2,948/10,000 at .70 m; 3,958/10,000 at 1.10 m; 4,458/10,000 at 1.50 m. Each is within 2.5 percentage points of target. Tests also check factor direction, bounded luck and real fence indices.
17. Existing save/import/export tests remain in the suite. New tests check RPG-state persistence after explicit recovery, no duplicate quick result, immutable opening scores, and stabling charge idempotency. Single-save behavior is retained.
18. Remaining: the exhaustive 82-case acceptance matrix; the full trait audit and cross-system connections; all supplied exact narrative lines; independent physical-exam sensitivity population tests; comprehensive country/continent itinerary and overnight-availability testing; browser testing of every discipline, jump-off, overnight and veterinary route; physical touch-device validation. Do not mark this draft as the completed brief or merge it on the strength of the statistical tests alone.
