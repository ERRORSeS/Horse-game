# Complete competition, individuality and veterinary update

The replacement PR contains the entire update against main, including the jump-off, Quick Compete and rail-weight fixes previously proposed in unmerged PR #147. No merge of #147 is required.

1. **Qualification.** Opening-round eligibility uses exactly zero total penalties and no elimination, including time penalties. The earlier NPC scoring loop processed earlier starters without restricting its jump-off pool. It now checks the saved qualifier IDs. Nonqualifiers receive only a spectator action.
2. **Opening results.** No intentional first-round reroll existed in the inspected path; inappropriate extra NPC jump-off records made the results misleading. Opening score snapshots, IDs, flags and standings are saved separately. Jump-off fields hold only qualified performances. The Silver/Storm/Eclipse/Nova/Ruby example has an exact ranking regression. Changing player flags cannot change ranking.
3. **Five RPG values.** Jump-off creation previously constructed default 50-point state. It now copies the actual tension, focus, confidence, energy and timing. Explicit quiet recovery or warm-up applies bounded changes once. Browser evidence: 32/82/88/69/76 became 26/84/89/74/76 after quiet recovery, survived reload, and entered the playable jump-off unchanged.
4. **Quick Compete.** The former normal registration path entered live sessions without a one-click driver. Quick Compete now runs the existing scene resolver, scoring and NPC continuation. Eventing resolves dressage → cross-country → stadium. The saved stage/result guards prevent rerolls and repeated awards. Quick first rounds can lead to either quick or interactive jump-offs. Full-state serialization occurs after the synchronous performance, rather than after every simulated decision. Mode selection has authoritative state so stale UI selections cannot disagree with the button.
5. **Height calibration.** Round-level baselines are 30% below 1.00 m, 40% from 1.00 to below 1.30 m, and 45% at/above 1.30 m. Supported heights are unchanged; .95 uses the lower band, 1.25 the middle band, and outside heights use the nearest outer band. A zero-fence course produces no invented fence or rail.
6. **Personality.** A centered normalized score contributes up to ±3 percentage points: 20-point budget × 15%. Existing Careful Front End and Careful but Slow reduce rail risk; Rushes Fences and Trails Hind Legs increase it. Other contextual behavior rules affect suitable scenes without inventing new permanent traits.
7. **Tack.** The 15% share contributes up to ±3 percentage points. The live signal reads actual saddle fit, comfort, equipment condition and control; price is not an input. Sensitive horses react more strongly to discomfort while staying within that budget. Existing ownership, permitted equipment and eligibility checks remain in place.
8. **Mood.** A centered normalized score contributes up to ±4 percentage points (20-point budget × 20%). Motivated/focused states and distressed/tired states have different effects. Medical discomfort is a separate temporary overlay and does not rewrite permanent temperament or Bond/QOL.
9. **Luck and rails.** The deterministic per-entry/per-round uniform ±3-point luck draw is saved in the pole plan. One whole-round event allocates actual fence indices. NPC jumping also uses a whole-round plan. Refusals and time penalties remain separate; the result displays rails, jumping penalties, time penalties and total penalties. No hidden luck value is exposed in player UI.
10. **Trait audit.** All 55 existing `T2_TRAIT_RULES` have a registered description, trigger, systems, modifiers, narrative, frequency and conflict list in `trait-effect-audit.json`. Each has an eligible/ineligible trigger regression. Existing training learning/education and exclusions are retained; relevant competition, warm-up, grooming, clipping, loading, unloading, settling and examination handling use those rules. The 60/30/10 narrative framework does not add another illness or fallen-rail roll. Veterinary handling is visible, records extra minutes, affects an active show clock where applicable, and is idempotent.
11. **Transport individuality.** Profiles retain established histories and equivalent existing numeric values. The supplied loading weights sum to 130 and are normalized as weights. Travel tolerance is drawn independently; the unspecified nervous-traveler remainder is 12%. Actual loading weights reflect category, familiarity, confidence, fatigue, environment, traits and staff support. Unloading confidence and venue adaptability affect their own stages. Settled trips improve coping slowly; difficult trips create a bounded, decaying expectation. Reopening a menu cannot reroll the profile or repeat the experience benefit.
12. **Overnight planning.** More than five hours requires a night; exactly five does not. One/two/three nights cost $65/$120/$175, plus $55 per extra night. Booking checks the complete itinerary, balance, overlap-based stall availability and fixed Calendar dates before charging. Reservations, receipts, transport legs, per-night evening/care/rest/morning stages and care records persist. Country and continent trips require longer buffers; continental travel has timed road/long-distance/arrival legs. Impossible arrivals and unavailable stalls fail without charges; reservations release on return. Browser QA booked and reloaded a three-night 48-hour itinerary, then completed all nights with no further fees.
13. **Physical exams.** General exams read actual active conditions, current symptom intensity and asymptomatic flags. Detection uses 40/70/90% visibility bands, zero when there are no observable symptoms, and existing condition-specific investigation where applicable. Findings correspond to the actual condition and recommend targeted testing without automatically confirming a hidden diagnosis. Existing targeted diagnostics, prescriptions, hospitalization and recovery remain in the original medical engine.
14. **Medical mood.** One seeded daily quantile implements conditional 15/40/70/90% discomfort bands. Changes after treatment/exertion reuse that quantile instead of rerolling. Resolved/asymptomatic conditions clear the overlay. Permanent personality and the underlying mood system remain intact; show/profile displays can show Uncomfortable. Browser general examination recorded cough/nasal discharge and an uncertain concern, with no fabricated diagnosis.
15. **Files and functions.** `show-session.js` handles pool restrictions, score snapshots, state carryover and live UI. `showjumping-round.js` handles bounded centered weights and real fence counts. `health-engine.js` adds general symptom observation. `competition-repair.js` retains the initial repair integration. `competition-completion.js` completes contextual traits, booking/reservations, individual logistics, narratives, mode controls and examination handling. `competition-narratives.js` preserves all 92 supplied lines. `ui-state.js` respects authoritative mode selections. Tests, asset versions and the audit/report are included.
16. **Population checks.** At least 10,000 seeded neutral rounds per tier: .70 m 2,948/10,000 (29.48%); 1.10 m 3,958/10,000 (39.58%); 1.50 m 4,458/10,000 (44.58%). All are within 2.5 points of 30/40/45%. Independent physical-exam populations: mild 415/1,000; moderate 718/1,000; obvious 902/1,000; no symptoms 0/1,000. Tests check factor directions, sensitivity, bounded luck, real fence indices and conditional medical-mood bands.
17. **Persistence and compatibility.** Tests cover five-value persistence, immutable opening scores, jump-off mode switching, quick reroll guards, single awards, existing saving/import/autosave, profile history, reservation overlaps, failed-booking atomicity, multi-night care, venue familiarity and repeated booking/experience. All 83 new integration checks (14 repair checks and 69 completion checks, including 55 individual trait cases) pass, alongside 121 core checks, 25 expansion checks, the integration/longevity suites and the complete existing regression suite. `script.js`, `care-fixes.js`, `care-sales.js`, `behavior.js`, `tack.js`, `management.js`, `pasture.js` and `training2.js` remain byte-for-byte unchanged from main. Browser QA covered all four disciplines, interactive choices, quick scoring, both jump-off eligibility paths, veterinary findings, international booking and overnight reload. Console inspection returned no errors. A phone-width choice resolved successfully; 390- and 1280-pixel layouts had no horizontal overflow.
18. **Practical limits.** Physical touch hardware and other browser engines were not available; phone-width interaction was tested in the in-app browser. The original game has no two-phase jumping implementation, so this update does not invent one or label it as a jump-off. International itineraries are fictional game logistics and preserve existing eligibility rules; no real legal paperwork or current border regulations are modeled. The stable calendar remains separate from the show clock, preserving results without calendar skips. Existing browser-storage capacity limits still apply, with the verified single-save failure behavior retained.
