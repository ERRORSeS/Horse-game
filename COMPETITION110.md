Competition settings now support a saved custom fault percentage from 0 to 100. This is the chance per fault opportunity, not the chance of one fault over an entire round. Zero disables random execution, pole and refusal faults; 100 guarantees an applicable fault. Time penalties, equipment eligibility and existing results are unchanged. Presets remain available: Easy is 45% below Normal, Hard is 30% above Normal. Training probabilities remain unchanged.

Aggressive uses 0.82 of Balanced moving time; Competitive uses 0.94. Aggressive automatic course choices prefer shorter lines without looking ahead at random outcomes. Refusals, rhythm and drift retain their actual recovery delays. Eventing pace now follows the same ordering.

Default generated fields increase from 17 to 50 total combinations with prestige. Eligible real career entrants determine the actual field; fixed team, Games, specified starter counts and custom shows retain their rules. Unentered future events in saves adopt these defaults.

Official ranking results can score when optional career progression is disabled. Loading repairs known eligible zero-point results once using stored scoring rules or the real catalogue. It does not replay shows, pay prizes, invent starts or rank local schooling shows. Weekly ranking release timing remains unchanged; newly joined NPCs begin without points until they earn them.

Lesson-horse jumping scope uses a stable saved draw capped by breed limits. Scope above 1.20m occurs only exceptionally at 3-star lesson facilities and more often at 4–5 stars. Larger scope is progressively rarer. This changes potential, not current schooling, rider ability or age eligibility. Existing lesson horses receive the selection once; owned horses and active leased copies are unchanged.

Validation: native browser checks for 0/25/100%, a real zero-fault round, settings/reload, pace, incident delay, prestige/fixed counts, official NPC scoring without progression, score repair without payments, and 20,000 deterministic lesson scope selections. Calendar jump-off, preset difficulty, style timing, dressage/eventing browser flows and protected marking sources also checked.
