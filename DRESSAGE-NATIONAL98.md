# Realistic dressage and confirmed national entries

The earlier shared dressage scale made full training and clean execution worth 95–100% too readily. The revised common judge centers accurate, well-prepared, highly trained work in the 70s/80s. Skill, execution, preparation and actual deductions still matter. Seeded test form and movement expression add modest variation; exceptional expression can earn 9–10, but an entire 90%+ test requires unusually strong work and form. The judge is identical for player and NPC, including manual and automatic performance. Tied dressage percentages still share placing. Previously completed saved results are not rescored.

Across 2,000 simulated tests of twenty movements at maximum training, perfect execution and full preparation, mean percentage was 85.13; only three tests (0.15%) reached 90%+. Six of 40,000 individual movements earned 10. Ordinary rides additionally incur their actual execution errors and preparation effects. A native browser complete manual Grand Prix placed first against its actual NPC field and survived verified save/reload. This demonstrates that winning remains attainable without routine 96–100% tests.

National entry could reject a confirmed main-squad combination with “filled its quota with stronger qualified combinations.” An older selection formula ran after the active federation nomination had already been checked. Entry now honors the verified nomination rather than rerunning that older score contest. Existing nomination, country, division, horse, health, qualification, team-size, deadline, equipment and entry-fee checks remain in force. A reserve without a main-event nomination remains blocked. Main squad membership still does not automatically confer Games qualification.

Validation: 21 federation progression scenarios, including a real careerEnter call for a nominated main member against artificially higher legacy candidate scores and rejection after demotion to un-nominated reserve; native actual calendar Enter button, registered horse/country/main role and verified reload; four dressage judging scenarios and the 2,000-test population; native dressage result/reload; interactive/eventing competition, ranking points and Year 1 trial regressions. Evidence is in validation97/. The full historical suite was not rerun.
