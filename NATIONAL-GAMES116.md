National opponents and team scoring

The fresh-career regression reproduced a team field containing only the four home riders. Foreign squads had actually been selected, but ranking91EventRoster held a rider index captured before lazy opponent selection added those riders. Nomination lookups and the final field now refresh that index, so selected rivals reach the competition.

Foreign trial candidate lists now count combinations that meet the actual class schooling requirements rather than any twelve age-eligible riders. New recruits are generated at an appropriate standard and must pass the same eligibility checks; the generation loop is bounded. Ordinary squad selection still requires real completed trial/results evidence.

Incomplete foreign teams are removed after final qualification checks instead of entering as partial national teams. Existing country quotas, full squad sizes, 50/100 category limits and player nomination rules remain. Ordinary team competitions now have opposing classified countries and therefore earn their normal team points. Ranking publication remains weekly. Completed historical single-country results are not rerolled into invented contests.

On load, eligible unfinished first-round national-programme shows containing only one country restore their canonical invited-country list and append missing qualified opponents. The player entry, already recorded efforts and scores are preserved. Completed or awarded shows and jump-offs are not rewritten.

Olympics and Youth Olympics

Games and Games qualifiers use international age-division pools instead of treating the host country as the only source of individual opponents. Actual past rival selection trials can prepare Games squads too, subject to the existing time guard; browsing future shows does not simulate future results.

Youth Games allocated horses are now adult, class-suitable sport horses with consistent age, potential, scope and available health state. Random founder breed/health/retirement data can no longer make a newly supplied Games mount unridable or below the class level. The rider's owned horse is unchanged. Games retain their existing championship medal and ranking-point rules.

Validation

- Fresh career: eight actual monthly worker skips, six full national squads and real team-ranking points.
- Senior opposition: complete foreign squads in jumping, Grand Prix dressage and eventing.
- Saved open home-only round: full rivals restored without changing the player identity or recorded choices; reload verified.
- Senior Games: actual regional-to-international results, application and both selection trials, earned Games entry, complete opposing teams, individual/team results and medals, then reload.
- Youth Games: real opening/national/Games trials, earned squad place, allocated mount, opponents from 21 countries, result/medal and reload; 60 newly generated allocations also checked for riding and schooling suitability.
- National team UI/history/point reconciliation, no solo-team point inflation, no future-trial fabrication, main-squad native entry and protected marking-source regressions passed.

Scripted clean rides in the reachability tests exercise actual entry, scoring, result writing and selection. Qualification flags and selection membership are not forced.
