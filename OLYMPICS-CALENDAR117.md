Olympics discovery and legacy settings

The senior and youth Games existed under the World Arena Games names and only appeared in their scheduled edition's calendar. Monthly views, old eligible-only filters and browsing the current non-Olympic year made them difficult to find. An older save missing the games preference could also hide championship events entirely.

The existing events and qualifiers are now labelled Olympic Games / Youth Olympic Games. IDs, four-year cycles, qualifications, quotas, results and medals are unchanged. Calendar now always displays an Olympics & Youth Olympics section with next edition years/dates and direct buttons to that edition's actual events and qualifiers. The buttons clear conflicting filters and open the season list, so all Games classes fit on the first page. The Class type filter includes Olympics & Youth Olympics.

Senior editions remain Years 4, 8, 12…; youth editions remain Years 1, 5, 9…. The next-edition display rolls forward after the current edition finishes and remains visible outside edition years or booking windows. Qualification and normal booking deadlines still apply to entry.

A missing legacy championship setting now defaults to enabled in both scheduling and entry. An explicitly disabled setting stays disabled; the calendar explains it and provides an Enable championship events button. Browsing future editions creates no future trial/results records and advances no game time.

Validation

Native calendar buttons showed senior jumping/dressage/eventing events and the youth event plus qualifiers despite stale hunter/kids/tier/eligible/prestige filters. Tests cover non-edition years, post-edition rollover, explicit disabled setting and enable button, missing legacy preference, save/reload and unchanged meet count while browsing future editions. Existing full senior and youth qualification/entry/result/medal browser journeys pass with the new labels.
