# Ranking consistency and first-year tryouts

Standings rebuilt by the bounded population policy discarded best-rank metadata. They now preserve recorded best positions through rebuilding, movement and reload. New zero-point contenders show “Best —” until they establish a position with points. Filtering pools also renumbers positions before publication so rank history cannot retain gaps from excluded competitors.

An older NPC entry policy awarded only 25%, 50% and 75% of normal points on the first three scoring starts. This turned some low-tier wins into one point. NPCs now receive the same tier, placing, performance and field-strength award as the player from their first eligible result. They still enter at zero and cannot earn points before their debut. Pool limits, five-per-week growth and actual-calendar-only scoring remain intact.

A one-time save repair recomputes verifiable real NPC results using frozen scoring rules, a matching calendar class or the known sanctioned-custom rules. It excludes private backstories, hidden circuits, local/unranked results, player results and pre-debut records. It republishes the existing ranking cutoff without replaying money or horse development. Newly completed meets retain their scoring rules for future traceability.

Ranking details list the actual credited points within the selected board's published period, including the best-30 world limit and seasonal limits. Horse/combination details use explicit rider and horse IDs, including IDs containing pipe characters. General horse career history is separately labelled and distinguishes unranked results.

Year 1 gains an additional January tryout (January 8–23 depending on division) alongside the existing May rounds and June squad selection. Applications open January 1. Eligible applications at least 48 hours before their opening tryout receive an immediate invitation, without needing an invented previous-year result. Rider age, nationality, ownership, schooling and medical checks still apply. A completed real trial supplies selection evidence; entry does not confer team membership. Later years keep their regular cycle. Existing selected seasons are not retroactively given new trials, while new opening-trial history survives selection and reload.

Validation includes four ranking scenarios, three opening-tryout scenarios, native Chromium standings/details/save-repair checks, actual calendar application/registration/reload, all four discipline scoring checks and the existing bounded-population, competition-history, schooling and twenty federation-progression scenarios. A two-month real-calendar simulation checked 484 scoring results and 43 wins across all four disciplines, and reconciled 661 published standings rows with their credited results. The annual federation fixtures run in Year 2 to independently verify the established two-round cycle; new Year 1 tests exercise the additional round. Evidence is in validation93. The complete historical suite was not rerun.
