# Automatic behavior preservation and very low rider control

Scheduled automatic work, legacy Auto Training and Quick Training preserve the horse's core behavior, breaking-in state and learned Rider Realism state. Their actual outcome, ordinary technical/riding skill progress, fatigue, medical exposures, stamina, reporting and scheduling transactions continue through the existing training engine. Automatic work cannot award recovery milestones or create learned behavioral setbacks.

For manual mounted work with Rider Realism enabled, a rider ability in 0–15 adds a significant communication-risk penalty. The increase is 10 percentage points with ordinary equipment, or 15 points with an equipped Pelham, double-bridle/Weymouth configuration, Flash Bridle, or actual owned/assigned crop or spurs. Equipment does not modify the neutral pole plan.

Using the listed equipment with very low control imposes a deterministic 7–10 point net session loss in Rider Trust in Aids, bounded by zero. The session saves its baseline, roll and recorded flag. Subsequent attempts, finishing and reloading cannot stack this penalty. Automatic training is excluded. Other skills and natural temperament remain separate.

Validation: 45 targeted checks passed across auto-training, rider-realism, behavior-recovery, tack-rider-aids and the new auto-behavior-rider suites. Includes actual scheduled skill/attribute progression with Rider Realism enabled and no behavior change, daily/monthly scheduling equivalence, save/reload transactions, mounted auto outcomes, all specified existing equipment IDs, the 15/16 boundary and a single 7–10 point session loss.
