# Jumping confidence and avoidance

Manual jumping with Rider Realism enabled uses the lowest rider ability: 0–15 selects a 3–6 point decline band; 16–20 selects 2–4. Skill 15 belongs to the lower band because the requested bands overlap. The same low-control experience increases Learned Fence Avoidance. This session envelope is additional to the normal resolved-event learning rules.

A saved session roll chooses the initial magnitude. More than three distinct resolved negative jumping incidents raises the envelope to the band's maximum. Multiple behavior/equipment messages about one attempt count once. Actual adverse outcomes and resolved communication incidents qualify; positive or merely informational observations do not. Automatic and Quick Training remain excluded.

Selecting Continue without addressing the concern, Crop punishment, or Spur punishment during a real jumping concern adds a separate 2–4 Jump Confidence loss once per session. Supportive choices do not. Saved keys and session flags prevent repeat penalties across clicks or reloads; journal changes and setbacks use actual recorded values. Staff do not end the session.

Validation: 37 targeted checks passed across rider realism, behavior recovery, automatic behavior preservation, tack compatibility and three new jumping groups. Coverage includes 0/15/16/20/21 boundaries, the fourth distinct incident, duplicate messages/replay, positive/unrelated/automatic exclusions, neglectful choice, save/load and existing protected-state checks.
